Privacy Policy - Three Days Emergency Tracker

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Three Days Emergency Tracker ("the app") is published by Thomas Piperis ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

1. Information you provide

  • Household profile: household name, number of adults, teens, children and elderly members, pets, dietary restrictions and medical needs you choose to enter. This is used only to calculate your emergency supply list.
  • Supply list: items, quantities and expiration dates you track.
  • Optional account: if you create an account or sign in (email or Sign in with Apple), we receive your name and email address to operate your account.

2. How information is stored

When you use an account, your household profile and supply list are stored with our backend service provider (Base44) so they are available to you across sessions. Some information is also kept on your device so the app remains usable offline.

3. AI-generated supply lists

When you generate or regenerate a supply list, the household details described above (member counts, pets, dietary and medical needs) are sent to an AI service to produce the list. We do not send your name or email for this purpose.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Subscriptions (Yearly Premium and Monthly Premium) are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate subscription status. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase status. We never see or store your payment card details or Apple ID billing information.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your information for advertising or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

6. Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app sends notifications about supply expiration and readiness. You can disable them at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

You can edit your household data in the app at any time. In the app under Settings you can choose "Delete Household & Data" or "Delete Account" to permanently delete your information. You may also email us to request deletion.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the effective date above when we do.
